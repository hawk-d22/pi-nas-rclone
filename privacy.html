Privacy policy Last updated: 17 November 2026 pi-nas-rclone is a personal configuration of rclone used by a single person, its owner, to synchronise their own Google Drive with their own home server. What data is accessed With the owner's permission, the app reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive, for the sole purpose of keeping a copy on the owner's home server in step with Drive. How the data is used and stored Files are copied only between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's own server. They are not sent to any other service or person. The app has no servers of its own and collects no analytics, advertising or tracking data. Google user data is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's server. Other users The app is not offered to anyone else. No other person's data is accessed. Removing access Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.
